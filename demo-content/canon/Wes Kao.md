# Wes Kao

Co-founder of Maven, writing on communication, influence and managing up.

**Why I trust them:** precise, practical advice on how to get buy-in, push back and communicate with executives.

**Lens:** influence is earned through clear recommendations, anticipating objections and keeping alignment alive after the decision.

**Use for:** presenting to the CEO, getting buy-in for a plan, saying no without damaging relationships. See [[annual-planning]], [[first-90-days]].

**Blind spots:** written mostly for managing up to one boss. Less on handling several executives at once or on marketing strategy itself.
