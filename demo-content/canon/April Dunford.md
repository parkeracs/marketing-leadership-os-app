# April Dunford

Positioning expert and author of Obviously Awesome and Sales Pitch.

**Why I trust them:** the most rigorous, widely used method for B2B positioning, built from years of hands-on work with software companies.

**Lens:** positioning is a sequence: competitive alternatives, then differentiated capabilities, value and best-fit customers, with the market category last.

**Use for:** positioning and messaging decisions, sales narratives, and judging whether a category play is worth it. See [[positioning]].

**Blind spots:** less to say about brand, demand generation or running a marketing team. The method assumes you can reach and interview customers.
