# Kieran Flanagan

Marketing executive writing on AI, marketing systems and the changing CMO role.

**Why I trust them:** systems-focused thinking on AI in marketing, rather than generic tool takes.

**Lens:** AI splits the marketing job into a technical growth half and a narrative brand half. Few leaders are strong at both, and the real constraint is hiring judgement.

**Use for:** AI in the marketing function, team design and how marketing roles are changing.

**Blind spots:** "the CMO role is dying" is a provocation, not a description of every market or company stage.
