# Topline (Pavilion)

Pavilion's operator-led media for senior go-to-market leaders: a newsletter and two podcasts.

**Why I trust them:** current, operator-written perspectives from CROs, CMOs and founders, including real debate between them.

**Lens:** go-to-market in the age of AI: the revenue leader's view of the whole team, not marketing alone.

**Use for:** the CRO's and CEO's view of marketing, current GTM and AI trends, and peer perspectives that balance the more framework-led sources. See [[annual-planning]], [[pipeline-and-lead-management]].

**Blind spots:** leans towards sales and the CRO, and towards US venture-backed SaaS. News and hot takes date fast, so treat specifics as volatile and check them before quoting.
