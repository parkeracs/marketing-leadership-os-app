## Why it works

- **A wide-eyed cat stops the thumb.** In a feed full of dashboards and screenshots, a startled cat on an electric purple background is the last thing you expect from an SEO tool.
- **The copy keeps the joke going.** "Meow that's a good content strategy" and "don't be demure" borrow internet slang of the moment, then pivot straight to a plain benefit question: plan, write and optimise content in one place.
- **The offer is low-risk and obvious:** "Try It Free", a 7-day trial and a clear arrow. The playful creative carries a very direct ask.

## Copy

- Post text: "Meow that's a good content strategy…" leading into a 7-day free trial of the content tools.
- On the creative: "What if you could plan, write, and optimize your website content in one place?" with a "Try It Free" button.
