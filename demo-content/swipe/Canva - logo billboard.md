## Why it works

- **The format is the joke.** A giant Canva logo bursts out past the billboard's frame, so the ad acts out the classic client request "make the logo bigger" instead of just saying it. You get the punchline before you've read a word.
- **It sells a B2B feature with a shared pain.** Every marketer and designer has heard "make the logo bigger". Tying that to Brand Kit ("stay on brand") turns an inside joke into the product's reason to exist.
- **Small details reward a second look:** comment tags ("Holly", "Tom") borrowed from Canva's own editor, and installers mid-job, make it feel like a live edit.
