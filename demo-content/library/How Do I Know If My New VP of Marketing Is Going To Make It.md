> [!summary] Principles
> - **The CEO's scorecard for a new marketing leader is written in the first 90 days.** Lemkin lists seven signs, and every one is visible early: a lead or pipeline commit, hands-on work, sharper positioning, early wins, talent upgrades, command of the numbers, and trust from sales.
> - **Own a number from day one.** Committing to MQLs, SQLs or pipeline is the signal that you are doing the job. Strategy without a commit reads as a red flag.
> - **Sales trust is a 60-day test.** Meet the sales leader weekly and align on pipeline goals. If the head of sales doesn't trust marketing within about two months, the CEO concludes something is wrong.
> - **Positioning work counts only if it shows up in the field.** The CEO looks for sharper narratives on the website, in sales decks and in campaigns, not a framework deck.
> - **Upgrade the bench fast**, with one or two A-players you have worked with before or, without budget, a cost-effective agency or contractor.

> [!warning] Numbers and tactics ⏳ as of 2025-08
> - Jason Lemkin (SaaStr, writing to CEOs) says the best new VPs of Marketing commit to a number, such as MQLs, SQLs or pipeline, in week 1 and show progress against it by week 4.
> - Lemkin says CEOs should expect a new VP of Marketing to produce clearer narratives on the website, in sales decks and in campaigns within 60 days.
> - Lemkin expects a new VP of Marketing to show real wins inside 90 days: a lead spike, a campaign that moves pipeline, visible brand lift, or a re-engagement programme that revives dead deals.
> - Lemkin tells CEOs to expect meaningful progress from a new VP of Marketing within one sales cycle, and not to wait a year before acting.
> - Lemkin adds that AI agents in the marketing stack shorten the time it takes a CEO to see whether a new VP of Marketing is working, and that the new VP should configure those agents personally at first.

## Notes
- Written to CEOs, so read it as the test you are being marked against. It is the clearest canon statement of what a CEO watches for in days 1–90.
- Seven signs in order: lead commit, hands-on early, positioning and messaging, early wins, recruit talent, know the numbers, align with sales.
- Pairs with [[If Your VP Hasnt Done Some Key Part of the Job by Day 90 They Never Will]] and [[The 1 Clear Sign a VP Will Fail]].
- Blind spot: assumes a pipeline-led SaaS motion where marketing owns lead gen. Brand-led or PLG roles need a different first commit.
- US venture framing; flag before applying to bootstrapped or non-SaaS contexts.
