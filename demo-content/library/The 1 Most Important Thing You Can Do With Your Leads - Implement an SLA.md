> [!summary] Principles
> - **The follow-up SLA beats stage taxonomy.** Debates about MQL vs SAL, rejection rights and acceptance windows matter less, possibly all the way to mid-scale, than one rule: every lead gets followed up.
> - **The SLA must auto-reassign.** First touch within a set window, a second follow-up within a set number of days. Miss either and the lead goes to another rep automatically. A rep who won't work a lead has signalled it should go to a hungrier one.
> - **Reps cherry-pick, and scoring doesn't save you.** Reps gravitate to "hot" leads, misjudge heat even with scoring, and simply miss things. There is money in warm and lukewarm inbound.

> [!warning] Numbers and tactics ⏳
> - Lemkin's companion posts set the window at same day or faster (2023), and at within 24 hours or faster with round-robin before territories (2022). Treat these as his house rules, not benchmarks (as of 2024-01).
> - Says the SLA matters "maybe even all the way to $10m ARR", and cherry-picking starts at roughly $1–2M ARR (as of 2024-01).
