> [!summary] Principles
> - The most common way good positioning dies is a fumbled handover to sales.
> - In first calls, consistency beats customisation: you haven't done discovery yet. The first call's job is discovery plus your best standard story, which earns the customised second call.
> - Sales that wasn't involved in creating the positioning won't use it. Involve sales leadership in building both the positioning and the pitch.
> - Pitches built by marketing or agencies that have never pitched miss discovery points and objection handling. Her verdict on agency decks: gorgeous, fluffy nonsense.
> - Not every persona needs its own pitch. Sell value to the champion. Arm the champion to handle IT, procurement, security and legal objections later.

> [!warning] Numbers and tactics ⏳ as of 2024-02
> - April Dunford's (positioning consultant) diagnostic for positioning that never reaches sales lists four failure points: a belief that every pitch must be custom, sales never agreeing to the positioning, a marketing-built pitch that doesn't fit the sales process, and persona-by-persona pitch variants.
