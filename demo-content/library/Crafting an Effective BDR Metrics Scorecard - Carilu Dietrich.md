> [!summary] Principles
> - **Look beneath the team average, rep by rep.** It shows whether marketing's lead flow matches the number of reps. Hiring too far ahead starves reps; flooding them hurts conversion and slows follow-up.
> - **Build the scorecard as a causal chain:** attainment to goal (booked meetings), then volume of inbound received by type, then share of it actually worked, then speed to respond, then raw activity.
> - **Separate the inputs marketing controls from rep execution.** That's what turns "the leads are bad" into a question with an answer: did the rep get enough, work it, and work it fast?
> - **Work the account, not just the lead.** Multi-thread beyond the person who came inbound to qualify the right buyer before passing to sales. More engaged contacts usually means higher close rates and contract values.

> [!warning] Numbers and tactics ⏳ as of 2023-10
> - The example BDR scorecard Carilu Dietrich (hypergrowth marketing leader turned adviser) shares, from 6sense's BDR team, tracks booked-meeting targets, predictive-qualified leads, event leads, chat conversions, the share of leads worked, chat response speed, calls and emails, and contacts engaged per qualified account.
