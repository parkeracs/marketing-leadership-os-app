> [!summary] Principles
> - Positioning defines the underlying parts (alternatives, differentiated capabilities and value, best-fit customer, category). Messaging is how that is expressed across every marketing and sales context. Positioning is the input.
> - Many 'messaging problems' are really positioning problems.
> - A messaging document is non-negotiable. Without one, each new asset copies the last and the story drifts like broken telephone within months.
> - The homepage can't be where messaging lives. Its job varies by motion (free trial, demo, end user versus buyer in PLG-plus-sales), and plenty of other assets need messaging too.
> - Homepage teardowns by outsiders are mostly noise. What matters is whether it resonates with your buyers.

> [!warning] Numbers and tactics ⏳ as of 2025-03
> - April Dunford, on the Forget The Funnel podcast, says a messaging document should contain the tagline, three value points and the approved way to talk about each, so new assets draw on one source instead of copying the last one.
