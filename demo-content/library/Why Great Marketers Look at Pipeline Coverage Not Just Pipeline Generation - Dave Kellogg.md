> [!summary] Principles
> - **Marketing can't be "doing great" when sales misses plan.** Hitting model-driven pipegen targets is a me-oriented metric; the point of the pipegen machine is to give sales a chance to make the number every quarter.
> - **Measure starting pipeline coverage first, pipegen second.** Coverage = current-quarter pipeline at the start of the quarter ÷ new-ARR target.
> - **If pipegen is on plan but coverage is falling, the model is wrong.** Usual causes of model drift: lower win rates, longer cycles, smaller deals. Revisit the planning model's assumptions rather than celebrating pipegen.
> - **Build the plan around coverage targets by quarter**, with pipegen targets per source derived from them.

> [!warning] Numbers and tactics ⏳ as of 2024-09
> - Dave Kellogg (CEO and board member) says a typical target for starting pipeline coverage is about 3.0x, and most companies can't make plan from 2.4x.
> - In Kellogg's companion post [You Can't Eat Pipegen](https://www.kellblog.com/you-cant-eat-pipegen/), he says sales usually generates 20–40% of pipeline, including SDR outbound (as of 2025-02).
