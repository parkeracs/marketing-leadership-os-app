> [!summary] Principles
> - **Product, marketing and sales must help create the positioning,** or buy-in after the fact is very hard, and their input is what makes it good.
> - **Marketing and sales build the sales pitch together.** Without sales in the room it won't meet sales' needs and won't stick.
> - **Once the pitch is agreed, sales leadership drives the rollout to the sales team,** not marketing.
> - **Revisit positioning a couple of times a year;** the CEO (or the business owner at larger companies) has to drive any change.
