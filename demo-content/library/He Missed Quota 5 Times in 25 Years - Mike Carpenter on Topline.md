> [!summary] Principles
> - **Don't pay marketing to claim credit.** Carpenter, as CRO, killed a post-quarter spiff that paid marketing to back-tag deals as marketing-sourced: it inflated the MQL story and told nobody where to spend. Single-campaign source attribution is unknowable when buyers arrive through friends, site visits, events and calls.
> - **Measure account warming, not lead origin.** Replace "who sourced it" with "how many touches did it take for this account to recognise the problem", and point marketing spend at accounts where recognition is building.
> - **Warm territories before you staff them.** Marketing builds pipeline in a "virtual patch" of untouched accounts. A seasoned rep goes in only once that patch has enough pipeline. Marketing's job becomes ramp acceleration, not lead hand-off.
> - **Staff sales analytics heavily and run 90-day experiments** ("X-factors"): each must be measurable, losers get cut each quarter, winners get rolled out to the team.

> [!warning] Numbers and tactics ⏳
> - Spiff: marketing team paid for the 30 days after quarter close to tag untagged deals as marketing-sourced; Carpenter stopped it (as of 2026-08, describing his CrowdStrike/McAfee era).
> - Roughly 16 "correspondences" (not just emails) to warm an account to problem recognition (as of 2026-08).
> - Patch released to a new hire at ~3x virtual pipeline; ramp cut from a nominal six months (really ~18 months) to 90 days (as of 2026-08).
