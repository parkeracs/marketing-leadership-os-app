> [!summary] Principles
> - **Check the interview story against reality.** O'Neill tests whether what she heard while being hired matches what she sees inside, and asks why she was picked over the other finalists. The answer tells you what you were hired to do first.
> - **Reset the pipeline commitment in the first 90 days, with data, or own it forever.** Gordon starts with what the CEO and CFO have promised the board, the pipeline commit and its assumptions, then the budget and demand model. He reset marketing's expected contribution share at his new company. Leadership respected a data-backed "this is what we can meet." Expectations often come from generic analyst benchmarks that don't fit the business.
> - **Do those four checks in parallel, not in sequence:** business objectives and pipeline commit; budget and demand-gen model; team initiatives, sentiment and performance; alignment with sales and RevOps. If the budget-versus-target gap only surfaces at day 90, it's too late.
> - **Use the outsider window.** You'll never be listened to more, or have a better chance to propose a radical idea, than in the first 90 days.
> - **The best quick wins are already half-built and repeatable.** O'Neill accelerates projects the team had stalled for lack of a sponsor: an instant win with the team and the business. McCarthy wants a win that can be repeated, not a one-off.

> [!warning] Numbers and tactics ⏳ as of 2026-06
> - O'Neill, a CMO interviewed on Drew Neisser's Renegade Marketing podcast, used AI throughout her move into a new CMO role: preparing her final interview presentation, turning onboarding notes and transcripts into themes, and building AI "thought partner" personas of each executive to prepare for meetings with them.
> - Gordon, a CMO on the same episode, cites CMOs who expected a budget of 5–10% of revenue, found 1%, and still faced a 25% growth target. Anecdotal, but a reason to check the budget against the target early.
> - Gordon still runs the new-CMO listening tour in person, including with the top two SDRs and the best sales reps, and warns against delegating it to AI.
