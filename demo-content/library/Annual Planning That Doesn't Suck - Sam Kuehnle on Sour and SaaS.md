> [!summary] Principles
> - **Agree with the sales leader, before the offsite, how you'll present who drove revenue.** Kuehnle and his VP of Sales show two lenses side by side: last-touch lead source, and the department that worked the deal (marketing, BDR, AE). In a dual PLG and sales motion, both are true. The point is resourcing to hit the growth target, not credit.
> - **If you're asked to commit to a number you don't believe, ask for time and go back to the math.** Take your average quarter over the last four (variable spend, overhead, blended funnel conversion) and show what the target would really take.
> - **Own what isn't working in front of leadership and the board.** Candour earns more respect than defending a channel that has stopped.
> - **Watch for diminishing returns on your best paid channel.** Don't redline it; keep some "break glass" headroom and build other levers (owned and earned) before you need them.
> - **Push back on the CEO's channel-of-the-week.** A half-resourced test of a new channel fails, then gets blamed. Double down on what works, or resource the test properly.
