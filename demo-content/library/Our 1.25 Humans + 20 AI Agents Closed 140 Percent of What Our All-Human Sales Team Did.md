> [!summary] Principles
> - **Coverage is the hidden lead-quality problem.** Human sales teams answer only the inbound they like, often days late. Before arguing about lead quality, measure what share of inbound got any response at all.
> - **Concentrate qualified leads in your best closers.** Spreading leads "fairly" across mixed-skill reps is inefficient. Lemkin thinks this alone may explain half of SaaStr's gain.
> - **Automate first-touch qualification, follow-up and re-engagement. Keep humans for closing.** The agents create more at-bats. They don't close the deals.
> - **Be honest about attribution.** With a market tailwind and an org change at the same moment, nobody can isolate which lever drove the result.

> [!warning] Numbers and tactics ⏳
> - SaaStr responded to fewer than 40% of inbound leads with humans, often days late, versus 100% instantly with AI agents (as of 2026-04).
> - 1.25 humans plus ~20 AI agents closed 140% of the prior year's all-human result, with a strong AI-market tailwind (as of 2026-04; events and media business, not typical SaaS).
> - AI agents worked the entire database of ~10,000 past prospects, where humans had cherry-picked ~500 (as of 2026-04).
