> [!summary] Principles
> - **The best predictor of VP failure is whether they still do the work.** For a CMO, that means generating pipeline personally: writing, running experiments, building campaigns, being in the tools.
> - **"Strategy people" fail.** CMOs who delegate everything lose feel for what resonates, lose credibility with the team, and coach from theory.
> - **Doing gives leverage, not just output.** Hands-on work produces patterns, case studies and early warnings that dashboards show too late.
> - **The first 90 days reveal it.** Lemkin's test: does the new leader jump into the work, or set up meetings and build process and hide behind "building the team"?

> [!warning] Numbers and tactics ⏳ as of 2025-11
> - Jason Lemkin (SaaStr founder) argues that SaaS leaders need to keep doing hands-on work, not just manage, until at least $250M ARR and probably $1B.
> - Lemkin claims AI writing tools let a hands-on CMO out-produce a former team, and that CMOs who don't write themselves can no longer judge content quality.
