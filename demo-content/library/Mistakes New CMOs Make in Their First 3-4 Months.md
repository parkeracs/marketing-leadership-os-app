> [!summary] Principles
> - **Talk to customers before you're indoctrinated.** The internal pull is strong when you start, but direct customer conversations give you information the echo chamber doesn't have, and something unique to bring to the board and execs.
> - **Move fast on the leadership team.** Remove toxic team members quickly, and bring in one or two trusted former colleagues to drive change. The longer issues fester, the bigger the risk.
> - **Share a direction early, even half-formed.** The first board meeting comes fast. Everyone wants your general priority and direction almost immediately. The reasons you joined and the risks you spotted are a fair first framework.
> - **Architect a drumbeat of visible early wins, and market them.** Short-term wins buy time for the long-term work. Don't over-prioritise the long term, or what *you* think matters over the CEO's passion area.
> - **Keep checking in with the CEO, CRO, CFO and CPO.** Ask whether you're communicating enough, at the right level of detail. They need more contact at first.

> [!warning] Numbers and tactics ⏳ as of 2023-02
> - Carilu Dietrich (former Atlassian CMO) says the classic new-CMO mistakes happen in the first 3–4 months in the role, the window her list covers.
> - Dietrich cites an Oracle habit: regularly ask "how do we market marketing?" to enthuse the company about marketing's work, get credit for it and protect the budget.
