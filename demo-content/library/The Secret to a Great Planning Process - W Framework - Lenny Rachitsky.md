> [!summary] Principles
> - **Most bad planning comes from unclear roles:** who has a say and when, who delivers what to whom, who sets the timeline, who holds people accountable and who makes the final call.
> - **The "W" Framework, four steps:** (1) **Context**: leadership shares a high-level strategy; (2) **Plans**: teams respond with proposed plans; (3) **Integration**: leadership integrates them into one plan and shares it back; (4) **Buy-in**: teams make final tweaks, confirm buy-in and start.
> - **Teams propose, leaders integrate.** The team writes the first plan inside leadership's context, which is what creates ownership.
> - **Stay ahead of planning.** The team that was ready on day one of the quarter delivered impact faster and with higher morale.
