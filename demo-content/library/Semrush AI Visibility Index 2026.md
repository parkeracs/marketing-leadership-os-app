> [!summary] Principles
> - Mentions (AI names your brand) and citations (AI quotes your site) are separate metrics earned by separate mechanisms (brand authority and category fit vs content depth), so track both, per platform, never as one blended score.
> - AI visibility is built upstream, in your category's **Citation Core** (the third-party sites every platform defaults to), so manage how you are described there, and aim for category leadership, benchmarking only against your own vertical.
> - The **four layers of Brand Visibility**: Discoverability (SEO/Content), Clarity (PMM/Brand), Authority (PR/Partnerships/Affiliate), Trust (Social/Community/CS). Different owners, so it needs one named owner across them.

> [!warning] Numbers and tactics ⏳
> - Sources cited per response: ChatGPT 15.4, AI Mode 11.4, AI Overviews 9.2, Gemini 3.3. Visibility on one platform doesn't carry over to another (as of 2026-04)
> - US software Citation Core: G2, Capterra, TrustRadius, Forbes Advisor. G2/Capterra matter most on ChatGPT (as of 2026-04)
> - Survey of 481 marketers: 81% with fully integrated SEO + AI search teams report more AI traffic or leads, vs 36% with separate workflows (as of 2026-04)
> - Concentration rule of thumb: where the top 3 brands hold more than 70% of mentions, aim for positions 4–10; under 50%, a top spot is realistic (as of 2026-04)
