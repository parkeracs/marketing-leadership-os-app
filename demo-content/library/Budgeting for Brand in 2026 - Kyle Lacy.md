> [!summary] Principles
> - **Name a protected brand bucket in the plan,** or it quietly disappears into "more pipeline". Split the *whole* marketing budget (headcount + programmes) between direct demand and brand-plus-experimentation.
> - **Earn the brand share by delivering the demand share.** If the demand budget hits pipeline and revenue (with attribution), executives rarely demand proof for the billboard; if it doesn't, nobody cares about brand spend.
> - **Execs and boards ask two things of brand:** how much pipeline, and how fast it shows in the forecast. Have a point of view and show it isn't risking the revenue number.
> - **Measure brand when you must defend or grow it:** branded search, direct traffic and flagship-content engagement trended against demo requests and target-account pipeline. Correlation, not causation, is usually enough.
> - **The split moves with stage:** more demand-heavy early, more brand as you scale and defend position.

> [!warning] Numbers and tactics ⏳ as of 2025-11
> - Kyle Lacy (CMO at Docebo, previously CMO at Jellyfish) splits the whole B2B marketing budget, headcount and programmes, 70/30 between direct demand and brand-plus-experimentation by default. By stage, he suggests roughly 80/20 at $0–20M ARR, 70/30 at $20–150M+ ARR, and up to 60/40 at later-stage or public companies.
