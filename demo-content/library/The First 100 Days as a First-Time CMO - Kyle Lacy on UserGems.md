> [!summary] Principles
> - **Observe before you give feedback.** Lacy spent his first 30 days as VP Marketing going to meetings and giving no feedback, just observing the team. He then spent 60 days prioritising what the team needed to do.
> - **Cut the metrics, then align on them with sales.** His first structural fix was overhauling funnel reporting. There were too many definitions and tracked metrics, so he cut them to four or five. That became the basis of sales–marketing alignment.
> - **Install operating rhythms early:** a lightweight agile cadence for the team (daily stand-ups, a shared board), a weekly funnel-and-forecast meeting across sales, marketing and CS, and a regular exec meeting.
> - **Hiring is the CMO's core skill.** Who you hire in the first 100 days, and what you delegate, decides what the function can do.
