> [!summary] Principles
> - **Measure rollout by what's actually said on calls, not by training attendance.** Conversation intelligence can show which reps are using the new deck and narrative, and which aren't.
> - **Measure efficacy as well as adoption:** connect narrative usage to CRM outcomes to see whether the reps using it win more, then use that proof to reinforce it with the rest of the team.
> - **Reps adopt faster when they see the link to revenue.** Share standout calls that use the new positioning, and coach the segments that lag.

> [!warning] Numbers and tactics ⏳ as of 2024-02
> - Gong, the conversation-intelligence vendor whose CMO Udi Ledergor wrote the post, reads slides shown on sales calls and tracks talk tracks, narratives and analogies through AI "trackers" and "initiative boards" (vendor features). This is how it checks whether reps are using a new deck.
> - Gong's enablement lead reports that Gong's win rates doubled when its reps used new messaging (vendor claim, Gong blog, as of 2025-03).
> - A Gong product marketer's workflow for a new segment: research it from discovery calls and champions' internal calls, search calls for the new slides to see how they land, then report week-over-week adoption against a quarter-end goal to executive sponsors (Gong on Gong podcast, date unknown).
