> [!summary] Principles
> - **Sidestep the attribution fight with a narrow, auditable definition:** inbound marketing pipeline is pipeline from a hand-raiser explicitly asking for a meeting with sales (contact-us, demo request, chatbot). No model required.
> - **Watch it weekly, ideally daily:** inbound qualified pipeline created (count and value, stage 2+), conversion through each stage (MQL→SQL→SQO→closed-won) and inbound coverage.
> - **Measure awareness spend by trend, not touches:** the share of qualified pipeline and of new ARR that comes from inbound, over 2/4/6/8 quarters. If brand, social and podcasts work, the inbound share grows.

> [!warning] Numbers and tactics ⏳ as of 2024-02
> - Ray Rike (founder and CEO of Benchmarkit, a SaaS benchmarking firm) advises taking the inbound pipeline coverage snapshot within the first 7 days of the quarter for deals under $10K ARR, and no later than day 21 for deals over $25K ARR. Use it to time the quarterly coverage check by deal size.
