> [!summary] Principles
> - **It's a two-way relationship.** First-time sales VPs often miss that the more they help marketing (webinars, lead scoring, following up), the more help they get back. Without that, marketing retreats to its own KPIs.
> - **Sit together and meet at least twice a week, once formally,** even with nothing on the agenda. If they don't, the CEO should start a three-way meeting until they run their own 1:1s.
> - **Pick one core funnel KPI before closed-won that both agree is marketing's #1 goal.** Two competing lead definitions mean two different goals.
> - **The VP Sales never blames marketing in public.** Lead-quality concerns go behind closed doors.

> [!warning] Numbers and tactics ⏳ as of 2021-06
> - Mutiny's (vendor) State of SaaS and Marketing Alignment report, cited by Jason Lemkin (SaaStr founder), found 66% of sales and marketing leaders don't feel positive after meeting each other.
