> [!summary] Principles
> - **Apply the fast SLA to hand-raisers only** (demo and contact requests). A call minutes after a demo request feels helpful; the same call after an ebook download feels intrusive. Hand-raisers are also where most pipeline comes from.
> - **Route only to reps who are available right now.** Working hours and PTO aren't enough; the rep has to be free at that moment. Availability is the difference between a 10-minute and a 2-hour response.
> - **Hold hand-raisers in their own queue ("a dam")** until a rep frees up, so they don't fall through to a slower, lower-priority routing branch. Back it with a human check for stuck leads.
> - **Count the SLA as met only when a human call is made,** not when an automated email goes out. Start the timer at routing, not form fill, because enrichment and sync take time upstream.
> - **Change management decides success.** Reps fear being chained to their desks and losing their share of leads; explain the money upside, roll out with grace, and show that distribution evens out.

> [!warning] Numbers and tactics ⏳ as of 2024-06
> - Justin Norris (Senior Director of BDR and Marketing Ops at 360Learning) set a 10-minute call SLA for hand-raisers (demo and contact requests), with a warning at 9 minutes; most 360Learning reps hit 90–100% compliance.
> - At 360Learning, lead-to-opportunity conversion for hand-raisers rose 40% after the speed-to-lead process launched.
> - In Justin Norris's experience, hand-raisers drive more than 80% of pipeline, which is why the fast call SLA is reserved for them.
> - About 40% of 360Learning's hand-raisers book a meeting themselves through an instant scheduler (Chili Piper) and are exempt from the call SLA; the call process covers the other 60%.
> - 360Learning sends after-hours and weekend hand-raisers to a separate team that distributes them evenly a minute before shifts start, so the first rep online doesn't get the whole pile.
> - 360Learning routes leads with Distribution Engine, which has a rep availability toggle; Justin Norris names LeanData, RingLead and Traction Complete as alternatives. Tool recommendations date quickly.
