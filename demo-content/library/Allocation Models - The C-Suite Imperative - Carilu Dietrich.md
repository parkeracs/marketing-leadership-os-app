> [!summary] Principles
> - **Make priority explicit with fixed numbers.** "If you had $100, how would you split it?" across regions, products, segments or approaches. 80/20 is a very different strategy from 50/50; without numbers every team makes its own trade-offs.
> - **Start from revenue contribution, then adjust** for growth rate, market opportunity, strategic importance or risk, and competitive windows. More art than science.
> - **Get the executives who disagree into one room.** When the president and CFO pull marketing in different directions, the fix is a shared allocation model; the CEO may need to make the call. Mature conflict up front beats conflict in the trenches.
> - **Cascade allocations.** Functions can allocate differently (product 70/30 new vs existing, sales and marketing the reverse) as long as the differences are agreed. Marketing can allocate by approach (e.g. demand gen / awareness / deal acceleration) or by audience.
> - **Don't spread the peanut butter.** Focused, bold bets beat evenly spread spend; repeat the agreed model constantly.

> [!warning] Numbers and tactics ⏳ as of 2025-01
> - Carilu Dietrich (hypergrowth marketing leader turned advisor) gives example allocations only: a marketing split of 60% demand generation, 20% awareness and 20% deal acceleration, and a 10–15% "innovation" allocation for AI work.
