> [!summary] Principles
> - Structural choices (where SDRs sit, where MOPs sits, how attribution is used) each help *or* hurt alignment depending on conditions. The episode is framed as "helps when… / hurts when…" for each one, not as one right org chart.
> - **RevOps as neutral third party**: when routing or ownership is disputed ("who does this lead go to?"), RevOps decides, not the sales manager.
> - Attribution is "directional insight, not a scoreboard". Pair it with one shared pipeline/revenue goal across all of GTM, so a shortfall from one source becomes everyone's problem to make up from another.

> [!warning] Numbers and tactics ⏳ as of 2025-04
> - The MKT1 Dear Marketers team argues that having SDRs report to marketing lets marketing own both inbound and outbound pipeline, which defuses attribution debates and speeds the move away from MQLs. The exception is referrals owned by partnerships.
> - The MKT1 team warns that keeping marketing ops inside marketing risks a separate "data language" and redundant tools compared with sales and partnerships; they favour a centralised RevOps team when systems need connecting.
> - The MKT1 team says an account-driven go-to-market only eases "lead quality" fights when sales and marketing have agreed the ICP and TAM, data sources, account tiering and rules of engagement, and it prioritises leads from engaged accounts.
> - On variable comp for marketers, Emily Kramer (MKT1 founder) says to do it for the whole marketing team or not at all. Grace Erickson (MKT1) says having no variable comp when marketing beats its goals breeds resentment and fuels attribution fights. The team sees a trend towards marketing variable comp tied to business outcomes.
> - The MKT1 team recommends not naming lead sources or stages after teams ("marketing-sourced", "MQL") and calling them what they are (inbound, outbound, demo request), to reduce territorialism.

Part 2 of 2; Part 1 (Ep 5, 2025-04-21) adds that rules of engagement must exist at two levels: **strategic ownership** (who owns awareness vs pipeline creation vs deal acceleration, which accounts) and **tactical handoffs**. Define who owns, supports and executes at each level.
- Failure mode flagged: teams enrich accounts and signals, then keep running the same tactics and still talk about qualified leads instead of engaged accounts.
- Ep 5 advice to marketers: tie work to sales' quota attainment, not MQLs.
- Works when there's one shared GTM pipeline number. Fails when attribution is used to crown a "winner".
